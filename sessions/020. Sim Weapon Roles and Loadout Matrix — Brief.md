# Session 020 — Sim Weapon Roles and Loadout Matrix — Brief

**Type:** feature
**Shape:** plan-backed
**Branch:** main

## Goal

Execute the plan file's "Sim weapon roles and loadout matrix" entry: turn weapon *strategy* in
`sim/` from logic hand-copied per archetype file into a declared, reusable unit per weapon
type, so missile and scout drone become usable by the sim, Energy sizing stops being a guessed
constant, and adding the next weapon after that doesn't mean writing a new archetype file.

  14, mortar range 20, `ENERGY_PER_GENERATOR_PER_SECOND` 0.15, plus the other sweep-picked
