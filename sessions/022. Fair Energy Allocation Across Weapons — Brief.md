# Session 022 — Fair Energy Allocation Across Weapons — Brief

**Type:** feature
**Shape:** stub-driven
**Branch:** main

## Goal

Fix the rules-core finding from Session 021: `planShots` (`src/shared/rules.luau`) pays out
the shared Energy pool to weapons in build order each tick, so a cheap weapon (cannon, 1
Energy) reliably starves a costlier one (mortar, 3 Energy) once both are drawing continuously.
Confirmed directly: a mortar alone on one generator gets 4 shots in 60s; add an auto-firing
cannon on the same generator and the mortar drops to 1 while the cannon gets 9. This session
makes weapon fire order/priority fair (or at least not systematically biased toward whichever
type happens to be cheapest and built first) by the end.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — Decide the allocation strategy

Options worth weighing, per Session 021's log and the plan file's "Notes" entry:

- **Round-robin / rotate the check order** each tick instead of always iterating in build
  order, so no single weapon permanently wins the race — cheap and simple, but doesn't
  guarantee a costlier weapon ever actually accumulates enough for its own shot if the pool
  is small relative to demand.
- **Priority-based allocation** — reserve Energy for a costlier weapon that's targeted and
  off-cooldown before a cheaper one gets to spend, roughly the inverse of today's order.
- **Borrow the sim's hold-fire concept** (`sim/loadout.luau`, `Kit.chooseTargets`'s
  `holdFire` parameter in `src/shared/archetypeKit.luau`) — a cheap weapon stands down once a
  costlier one exists and is actually plotted to shoot at something, so the costlier weapon
  gets first claim on the pool without needing a full allocation rewrite. This one already has
  a proven implementation to adapt from, just not wired into `planShots` for real play.

Also decide scope: real players only (`rules.luau`'s `planShots`, used by both
`MatchService`/`PracticeController`), or `server/Bot.luau` too (it has its own per-weapon
hardcoding and doesn't read `sim/loadout.luau` at all, per Session 020's deferral — pulling it
in here would be a second, related but distinct piece of scope).

### Phase 2 — Implement and verify

Whatever the chosen strategy, it needs shared-layer test coverage exercising the actual
starvation scenario (two weapons of different Energy cost, one generator, both wanting to
fire every cooldown) — the kind of direct measurement Session 021 did ad hoc via a throwaway
script, made a permanent regression test instead.
